Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Nival Operator runs on your own computer. Your email and calendar stay there: we don't run a server that receives them, we don't have an account system, and we don't collect analytics.

What the app accesses

When you connect Gmail with "Sign in with Google", the app asks Google for permission to read, label, archive and send your email (the gmail.modify permission; it cannot permanently delete email). With that it:

  • shows your inbox and conversations, and searches them when you ask;
  • marks conversations read and archives them when you ask;
  • sends the emails you write in the app;
  • checks new emails against the watch rules you set up, to show you a notification when one matches.

Where your data is kept

  • Your Google sign-in is stored on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's key store.
  • Your calendar events, watch rules, alerts and settings are stored on your computer.
  • Your email is fetched from Gmail when the app shows it and is not copied anywhere else.

The app talks only to Google (for Gmail) and, if you turn on updates, to GitHub (to download new versions). Nothing is sent to the developer.

Optional features you choose

  • Claude extension. If you install the Nival extension in the Claude desktop app, Claude can ask Nival to list or read your email when you ask it to in a conversation. What Claude reads is then handled by Anthropic under your Claude account and its terms.
  • Your own server. If you choose to run Mail and Watch through an operator server you set up on your own Cloudflare account, your Gmail sign-in and rules are kept on that server, under your control.

Sharing

We don't sell, share or transfer your data, and don't use it for advertising or to train AI models. Nival Operator's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • In the app, Settings › Disconnect Gmail removes Google's access and deletes the rules and alerts on your computer.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it and its data from your computer.
